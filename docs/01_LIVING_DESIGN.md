# MISERIA AETERNA: Living Design Document

The record of what the God has decided, what follows from it, and what remains open.
The constitution is `MISERIA_AETERNA_Master_Prompt.md` at the repository root. When this document and the constitution disagree, this document records the newer decision; the constitution's [SEED] items become canon only when they appear here as DECIDED.

Status words: **DECIDED** (the God's ruling), **PROPOSED** (the First Light's recommendation awaiting the God), **OPEN** (not yet asked or answered).

---

## The Seals Opened

### Seal I: The Silence (decided 2026-10-14)

**D-001 · The Hidden God. DECIDED.**
The God has never acted openly in Cinerea, and mortals have no proof. The God will intervene, but gently.

> The God's words: "Mortals' faith holds by will, and it is exactly the forge of a soul I want, a trial of will in that cruel world."

Consequence: faith is an act of will, not a conclusion from evidence. The game's central drama of the soul is a **trial of will** against a cruel world and a silent heaven.

**D-002 · The First Light walks in dreams. DECIDED.**
The First Light (the God's Herald) appears in mortal dreams. Proposed form: a lantern-bearer whose face the dreamer never remembers.

### Seal II: The Dark and the Dead (decided 2026-10-14)

**D-003 · The Tenebrae is canon. DECIDED.**
Accepted from constitution Part IV: unmourned death accumulates as darkness in the land (the Tenebrae), giving rise to the Unmourned, the Bargain, the Unlit, and the Strepitus. Remembrance and mourning are load-bearing forces of the world.

**D-004 · The Void is silent. DECIDED.**
> The God's words: "The Void is silent; mortals give it a meaning and a voice. Note the word silent. It is again THEIR will carrying that burden of silence."

The Void has no will and no voice of its own. Everything the dark becomes, it becomes through mortal will.

**D-005 · The Shore. DECIDED.**
The mourned dead rest on a shore only the God sees, each a lantern whose brightness is how strongly they are remembered. When the last person who remembers them dies, the lantern drifts into the quiet sea of the nameless: rest, not horror. The unmourned never reach the Shore; they remain in the land as Tenebrae.

**D-006 · Requiem. DECIDED.**
The God may grant rest to the dead of a place. If the living never mourn, the ground remembers and the dark returns. The God cannot do mortals' grieving for them.

### Seal III: The Measure of the World (decided 2026-10-14)

**D-007 · Scale. DECIDED.**
- Ultimate vision: a **Continent**, with an unmeasured sea beyond it.
- First year of development: one **Land**.
- First playable slice: one **Valley**.
- Each scale grows from the previous one without changing the world's laws.

**D-008 · Genesis and the Long Sleep. DECIDED.**
The God shapes the land and seeds the peoples; then centuries pass in accelerated, headless simulation, shown as the turning pages of an illuminated book; then the God wakes to watch a world that already has ruins, graves, and grudges.

### Seal IV: The Limit of the Hand (decided 2026-10-14)

**D-009 · The Gaze. DECIDED.**
Divine power is unlimited; divine attention is not. The God acts only where the God is looking, or upon a few Beloved souls kept in sight. Prayers outside the Gaze rise unheard, and the God can see that they went unheard.

**D-010 · The Weight of Wonder. DECIDED.**
Visible miracles leave Wonder in a region (fervor, fear, pilgrimage, cults, crusades, witch hunts). Subtle acts leave little. Wonder fades over generations; its consequences do not.

**D-011 · The First Law: the God cannot raise the dead. DECIDED.**
The God may prevent a death, give it meaning, or give the dead rest. The God may not undo it.

### Seal V: The Name (decided 2026-10-14)

**D-012 · The world is CINEREA. DECIDED.**
*Cinerea*, "the Ashen," is the world's true name, known only to the God and written on the first page of the Ledger. Mortal cultures name the world in their own tongues, and those names drift.

### Confirmations of the First Light's proposals (decided 2026-10-14)

The God answered "yes to all" to the six confirmations. The following proposals are now **DECIDED**: P-001 (the Forge of Will), P-004 (the Two Silences; the Bargain is heard, not offered, and written in the person's own hand), P-008 (the Nearness Law), P-009 (five Beloved), P-013 (the Carried Hearth and the fire of the First Night), and the resolution of C-001 (a short Long Sleep of roughly a century gives the First Valley a fully simulated past; the simulation is headless-capable from M0).

**D-013 · There was a fire before Genesis. DECIDED (nature OPEN).**
The sixth confirmation asked why the world is ashen. The God's "yes" is recorded as: Cinerea is made from what remained after a fire that came before Genesis. What burned, and whether the First Light may know it, remains OPEN.

### Seal VI: The Kindreds (decided 2026-10-14)

**D-014 · Humans alone. DECIDED.**
Cinerea holds only humans. All difference is culture, language, faith, and history. The giants of legend who built the ruins were older humans whose names were lost. Proposed and not contradicted: three or four Kindreds seeded at Genesis in the first Land.

**D-015 · The Sent. DECIDED (rules OPEN).**
> The God's words: "...and sometimes, as explicit mercy of the Lord, angel souls sent in the world to bring light, as simple humans."

Rarely, as an explicit mercy, the God sends a soul of the Heavenly Host into Cinerea to be born as an ordinary human. They are simple humans: mortal, without miracles, without proof. Their rules (memory, whether they can fall, what happens at their death, how rare they are) are OPEN; see P-018.

### Seal VII: The Ages (decided 2026-10-14)

**D-016 · The bounded climb on the open road. DECIDED.**
> The God's words: "Humans' minds are not limited, but the resources with which they build are thin, extremely thin, and loss of knowledge (libraries burning, etc.) is possible, so the maximum possibility is Advanced Gothic: proto-industrial, or already with steam."

- Invention is limited by **scarcity**, not by human ingenuity: thin ore, thin fuel, thin surplus.
- Knowledge can be **lost**: libraries burn, masters die without apprentices, crafts vanish. Dark ages are possible.
- The ceiling of the world's history is **Advanced Gothic**: proto-industrial, possibly early steam. Gunpowder and printing lie inside this band, because history reached them before steam (recorded as implied; the God may exclude either).

### Seal VIII: The Heart of the First Valley (decided 2026-10-14)

**D-017 · The Valley Below the Old Battlefield. DECIDED.**
The first playable slice is a valley downstream of a marsh where an army was never buried. The Tenebrae is present from the first hour. The shrine on the hill (the First Light's counsel) is kept unless the God forbids it; see P-019.

## Proposals Following From the Seals (PROPOSED, awaiting the God)

**P-001 · The Forge of Will** (from D-001). Each soul has **Will**: the capacity to hold a commitment (a faith, a vow, an Ember) under pressure. Will has a *strength* that drains under strain and recovers through rest, joy, and companionship, and a *temper* shaped over a lifetime. **Trials** (unanswered prayer, a child's death, hunger, torture, an unjust order, the Bargain) test a specific commitment against Will plus supports (Embers, people who stand with the person). Outcomes:
- *Tempered*: the commitment holds, and the person becomes both harder and more flexible. Mercy survives. The true forge.
- *Brittle*: the commitment holds only by hardening: fanaticism, cruelty in defense of the faith. Strong until the next great blow, then it shatters.
- *Bent*: the commitment changes shape: conversion, compromise, a new faith. Not a failure.
- *Broken*: the commitment fails: doubt, despair, Ashes, lost Embers, and vulnerability to the Bargain.

Which outcome happens depends on temperament (mercy and severity, pride and humility), on who stood with the person, and on how heavy the blow was. Faith is content held by Will, never a conclusion from evidence.

**P-002 · Sent Dreams and False Dreamers** (from D-001, D-002). The God's Voice acts arrive as dreams in which a lantern-bearer appears. The dreamer remembers the lantern and a distorted message, never the face. Mortals may also claim dreams they never had, or read ordinary dreams as sent; the Ledger marks which dreams were truly sent, and only the God knows. Each culture depicts the Lantern-Bearer differently (an angel, a dead mother, a hooded pilgrim, a skeleton with a lamp), and cults of the messenger itself may arise as heresies against the silent God.

**P-003 · The Small Mercies** (from D-001). The God's gentle toolkit: acts that could have happened naturally (a fever breaking a night early, a lost sheep found, a stranger's road bending past a door, a wind carrying seed). They leave almost no Wonder and no proof. The player still sees their consequences, because every divine act is sealed in the Ledger.

**P-004 · The Two Silences** (from D-004). There is a silence above (the Hidden God) and a silence below (the Void). Neither speaks. **Faith is will answering the silence above; the Bargain is despair answering the silence below.** The Bargain is *heard, not offered*: its "voice" is assembled from the person's own deepest want and its price from what they love most. In the Vigil it is written in the person's own hand. No one is forced; someone holding their hand at that moment can change the outcome. (This replaces the constitution's wording in 4.5, "the dark may offer a bargain.")

**P-005 · The Mind of the Dark from the Unlit** (from D-004). The Unlit have no lantern. When one dies, its will falls into the Void and colors the "voice" that later despairs hear in that region. The dark of each land is a confession of that land's despairs.

**P-006 · The Late Arrival** (from D-005, D-006). When one of the Unmourned is finally laid to rest, its lantern arrives at the Shore, perhaps centuries late. Those who waited for it may already have drifted into the nameless sea; or one old lantern may still be waiting, kept bright by a song. The player can watch it arrive.

**P-007 · The Mourning Debt** (from D-006). Requiem lowers a place's Tenebrae and lays its Unmourned to rest, but leaves a *mourning debt*. If no mortal mourning answers it within a span of years, the Tenebrae creeps back toward its old level.

**P-008 · The Nearness Law** (from D-009). The scale of the Gaze sets the grain of the Hand.
- From the **Cartograph**: broad acts only: seasons, a region's weather, a river's course, the reach of a plague.
- In the **Diorama**: a storm over a valley, a fire, a healing, a well.
- In the **Vigil**: a dream, a fever broken, a word, a touch.

The finer the mercy, the nearer the God must come.

**P-009 · The Beloved** (from D-009). A small, fixed number of Beloved souls (proposed: 5) on whom the God may act at Vigil grain wherever they are, and about whom tidings always reach the God. Taking a new Beloved when all places are full means releasing one.

**P-010 · Unheard Prayers** (from D-009). Prayers outside the Gaze rise and fade, and are kept in the Book of Prayers under a gray seal. The God can read them later; by then, nothing can be done.

**P-011 · Wonder, measured** (from D-010). Wonder from an act rises with the number of witnesses, the act's magnitude, and its strangeness (low for acts that could happen naturally). It spreads by rumor along roads and fades with a half-life of about a generation. Under the Hidden God it never becomes proof: each witness attributes it according to their own beliefs.

**P-012 · The First Law is universal** (from D-011). No power in Cinerea (God, Light, saint, witch, or Unlit) returns the dead to life. The Unmourned are not the dead returned; they are grief given a body. Mortals who claim to raise the dead are liars or deceived, and such claims can found cults. A death is final the instant it is written in the Ledger.

**P-013 · The Carried Hearth** (from D-012). Every hearth fire is an entity with a lineage. When a household moves, someone may carry a live ember from the old hearth to the new, as people really did. The new light is *the same fire*. The God can see a light that has burned for three hundred years across four homes. A sanctuary may keep the fire of the First Night, from Genesis itself, and its going out would be a catastrophe of meaning.

**P-014 · Ash and the next harvest** (from D-012). Burned land is more fertile for a few seasons, so a war that burns the fields brings famine first and, years later, an *ash harvest*. Ash customs may emerge (ash on the forehead in mourning or penance; the hearth's ash kept in a jar). The world's base tone is cinereous gray; warm embers are its accents.

**P-015 · Genesis sequence** (from D-008). The Deep (darkness, the Light speaks) → the Hand (shape the Land) → the Breath (set the climate's character through physical parameters) → the Kindreds (place the first peoples at their fires; they wake with no memory of being made, so the Hidden God holds from the first morning) → the First Night (the first Lights of the Living) → the Long Sleep (the pages of the illuminated book turn, each page a span of years with major Ledger events painted in its margins; the God may wake early or choose a length) → the Waking.

**P-016 · Knowledge is a carried fire** (from D-016). Every technique lives in people (a master's hands), in institutions (a guild, a monastery), or in books. It passes by teaching and copying, and dies when its last holder dies or its last book burns. A technique needs three things to appear: knowledge prerequisites, **material** prerequisites (ore, fuel, timber, surplus food), and social prerequisites (a patron, a workshop, peace long enough to try). In Cinerea the material gate is the narrowest. Progress consumes the land: forests become charcoal, hills become slag.

**P-017 · The Second Ash** (from D-016, D-012). If Cinerea climbs toward Advanced Gothic, its industry makes ash of its own: soot on cathedral stone, black smoke over the mine-towns, slag hills, children in the pits. The world named for ash becomes more literally ashen at its height. The same climb brings new light: printing (a book that cannot be burned out of the world), pumps that drain flooded mines, mills that end some famines. The first steam engines in our own history were pumps for mines; in Cinerea, the first may stand over a mine or under a cathedral organ.

**P-018 · The Sending** (from D-015). Proposed rules, to be decided:
- *How:* a Vigil-grain act at a birth within the Gaze. It leaves no Wonder: nothing visible happens.
- *What they carry:* nothing anyone could prove. A temperament leaning toward mercy, but not fixed. One Ember with no name, a homesickness for something they have never seen. Dreams of the Lantern-Bearer more often than others. Sometimes, in the Vigil, a word in their inner voice that no one taught them.
- *The Forge applies to them.* They can be tempered, made brittle, bent, or broken. Whether a Sent soul can hear the Bargain is OPEN.
- *At death:* whether they return to the Host or rest on the Shore among the human dead is OPEN.
- *Rarity:* whether the Host is finite (each Sent soul who stays on the Shore is lost to heaven) is OPEN.
- Mortals never know. The Ledger does. Some will be called saints, some burned as witches, and most will live small lives that bend a village's history.

**P-019 · The First Valley, sketch** (from D-017).
- A river comes down from the marsh through a narrow neck into the valley: a ford, a mill, eight to twelve households, a stone chapel and its graveyard, a road out.
- On the hill: a ruined watchtower from the battle's age, and beside it a small shrine over the grave of the one man who tried to bury the dead after the battle and died doing it, the valley's half-forgotten saint.
- In autumn, fog comes down the river from the marsh. Children dare each other to go to the reeds. The old people will not.
- **The battle is the first entry in the Valley's Ledger.** Because the war system arrives later (M5), the battle is seeded as a historical event at the start of the short Long Sleep: its dead are generated as real individuals with names and lives from two hosts, and lie in the marsh as the Unmourned. Then about a century of valley life is simulated. (Compromise Record to be written when M1 is planned.)

## Contradictions Found and Proposed Resolutions (constitution 0.7)

**C-001 · The Long Sleep is scheduled too late.** The roadmap (24.4) puts the Long Sleep at M7, but D-008 makes it the beginning of the game, and the First Valley's graveyard should hold real lives. *Proposed:* build the simulation headless-capable from M0, and give the M1 Valley a short Long Sleep (about 80 to 150 years) so every grave, ruin, and grudge in it was produced by simulation. The full Long Sleep (centuries, many settlements, language drift, the turning pages) stays later.

**C-002 · The Cartograph could let the God act everywhere,** which would undo the Gaze. *Proposed:* the Nearness Law (P-008).

**C-003 · A Hidden God can make the player's acts feel invisible.** *Proposed:* mortals never get proof, but the player always does. Every divine act is sealed in the Ledger, and its consequences can be traced, and offered by the Witness, as "the work of Thy hand."

**C-004 · Constitution 16.1 lists overt signs** ("speak a sign in the sky"). *Resolution:* overt acts remain possible under the Hidden God. They carry heavy Wonder and never prove anything; mortals explain them as comets, omens, sorcery, or saints.

**C-005 · Constitution 4.1 asked whether the God ever acted openly.** *Answered by D-001:* never. The "First Word" idea (from the rejected "God Who Spoke Once" option) is discarded. Faiths are born from interpreted events, sent and unsent dreams, catastrophe, and need.

**C-006 · Constitution 4.5 says the dark "offers" the Bargain.** *Proposed rewording by P-004:* the Bargain is heard, not offered.
